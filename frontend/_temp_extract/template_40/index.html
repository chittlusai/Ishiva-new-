iShiva Digital Technology crafts heirloom jewelry at the nexus of computation and craft. Drawing from sacred geometry and contemporary materials science, each piece is precision engineered, hand-finished, and ethically sourced.

Our atelier in obsidian black and 22K gold is both lab and sanctuary — a place where algorithms meet artisanship. We produce in limited quantities to honor material, maker, and wearer.
